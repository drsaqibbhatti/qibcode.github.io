Questions 자주 묻는 질문

What does the app prepare me for?

It is designed for Korean naturalization interview practice and the general knowledge commonly studied for naturalization preparation. It is an independent study aid, not an official government test or KIIP course.

Is this an official app?

No. QibCode is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Justice, Hi Korea, KIIP or another government body. Always confirm current legal and immigration requirements with official sources.

Does the app need the internet?

The free trial requires online verification. Full-access users need a connection for initial access verification, protected content download, purchase verification and some updates; downloaded full-access content is designed for offline practice afterward.

What data does the app use?

The app uses Firebase-backed account/access and device-registration data to manage protected content, trial usage and entitlement. Practice state such as Needed, Done, Deleted and resume information is stored locally on the device. See the privacy policy for details.

How much does it cost?

The launch plan is a 15-minute free trial followed by a ₩19,900 one-time full unlock. The final price shown by Google Play at purchase time controls.
